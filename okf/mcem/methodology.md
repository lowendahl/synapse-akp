# Definition

The Microsoft Customer Engagement Methodology (MCEM) is the end-to-end framework governing how Microsoft and partners engage customers across the full lifecycle. It defines five stages — Listen & Consult, Inspire & Design, Empower & Achieve, Realize Value, and Manage & Optimize — each with defined exit criteria, accountable organisations, and handoff points.

# FY27 Framework Principles

In FY27 the methodology shifts from product-centric to customer-centric execution:

1. **Frontier Transformation leads every engagement** — supported by Frontier and Core sales [Conversations](/mcem/frontier-transformation.md)
2. **Discovery Questions drive customer intent** — determining the fastest path to hands-on experience via [Discovery Questions](/mcem/discovery-questions.md)
3. **MCEM remains constant** — simplifies execution with conversations and fluency

# Stages

| Stage | Name | Accountable Org | Exit Criteria |
|-------|------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | [Listen & Consult](/mcem/stages/1-listen-and-consult.md) | ATU | Conversations and Qualified Pipeline identified |
| 2 | [Inspire & Design](/mcem/stages/2-inspire-and-design.md) | STU/DES | Solution validated, commitment secured |
| 3 | [Empower & Achieve](/mcem/stages/3-empower-and-achieve.md) | CSU | Deployment complete, initial value realized |
| 4 | [Realize Value](/mcem/stages/4-realize-value.md) | CSU | Business outcomes measured, expansion identified |
| 5 | [Manage & Optimize](/mcem/stages/5-manage-and-optimize.md) | CSU | Continuous optimization, renewal secured |

# Governance

MCEM is the single authoritative engagement methodology for all Microsoft customer-facing motions. Stage definitions, exit criteria, and accountability assignments from the FY27 MCEM Summary of Changes supersede any prior FY26 or earlier documentation.
