# MCEM Planning

The cross-org planning framework that governs how Microsoft teams coordinate across the customer lifecycle.

## Framework

* [Integrated Customer Planning](integrated-customer-planning.md) - The holistic ICP framework: Customer Plan = Account Plan + Consumption Plan + CSP(s) + Governance
* [Planning Taxonomy](planning-taxonomy.md) - Authoritative definitions of all ICP terms and concepts
* [MACC Consumption Planning](macc.md) - End-to-end MACC lifecycle: planning, quality gates, execution, and shortfall management

## Planning Disciplines

* [Account Planning](account-planning.md) - Always-on process for understanding customer and articulating Microsoft value
* [Customer Success Planning](customer-success-planning.md) - Accelerating value realization through CSP creation and execution
* [Consumption Planning](consumption-planning.md) - Pipeline sufficiency and execution against consumption targets

## Governance Rhythms

* [Customer Relationship Governance](customer-relationship-governance.md) - Customer-facing governance rhythm framework
* [QBR](qbr.md) - Quarterly Business Review: primary relationship governance mechanism
* [CSDR](csdr.md) - Customer Success Delivery Review: monthly execution governance
