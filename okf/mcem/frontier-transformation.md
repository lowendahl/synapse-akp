# Definition

Frontier Transformation is the organising principle for how Microsoft engages customers in FY27. It replaces the prior product-centric approach with outcome-based conversations where every account team engagement starts with Frontier Transformation discovery.

# Operating Model

- Every engagement begins with Frontier Transformation discovery
- Supported by Frontier and Core sales [Conversations](/mcem/methodology.md)
- [Discovery Questions](/mcem/discovery-questions.md) drive customer intent to the fastest path to hands-on experience
- CSU drives value expansion aligned to Frontier Transformation objectives in Stages 4 and 5

# FY26 → FY27 Change

| Prior State (FY26) | New State (FY27) |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Siloed priorities across solution areas | Frontier Transformation leads every engagement |
| Inconsistent customer experience | Unified outcome-based conversations |
| Product-centric selling | Customer-centric transformation |

# Relationship to MCEM

Frontier Transformation is not a stage — it is the lens through which all [MCEM stages](/mcem/stages/) are executed. ATU leads Frontier Transformation discovery in Stage 1; CSU drives value expansion against Frontier objectives in Stages 4–5.
