# MCEM Risks

Risk indicators and governance signals used to identify and mitigate threats across the MCEM lifecycle.

* [Renewal Rate](renewal-rate.md) - Lagging indicator of contract renewal health
* [On-Time Renewal](on-time-renewal.md) - Percentage of renewals completing within T-minus window
* [Recapture](recapture.md) - Recovery of at-risk or churned consumption/commitments
* [ACO Percentage](aco-pct.md) - Azure Consumed Outcome percentage against commitment
* [Pipeline Hygiene](hygiene.md) - Data quality and governance health of the pipeline
