# Definition

Discovery Questions are the structured qualification instrument used in MCEM Stage 1 to identify customer outcomes and determine the fastest path to hands-on experience. They replace ad-hoc qualification with a repeatable framework that connects customer intent to the appropriate MCEM Conversation and pipeline motion.

# Purpose

1. Identify customer outcomes through [Frontier Transformation](/mcem/frontier-transformation.md) lens
2. Qualify pipeline by driving customer intent to specific technology conversations
3. Create the bridge between Stage 1 (Listen & Consult) and Stage 2 (Inspire & Design)

# Exit Criteria Relationship

Discovery Questions are the primary mechanism through which [Stage 1](/mcem/stages/1-listen-and-consult.md) exit criteria are satisfied — specifically, "Conversations and Qualified Pipeline identified."

# LOB/Industry Orientation

Discovery Questions are structured by Line of Business and Industry vertical, ensuring customer engagement starts from business outcomes rather than technology capabilities.
