# Definition

Stage 4 — Realize Value is the fourth stage of [MCEM](/mcem/methodology.md). [CSU](/mcem/organization/csu.md) drives measurable business outcomes, deepens adoption, and identifies expansion opportunities aligned to [Frontier Transformation](/mcem/frontier-transformation.md) objectives.

# Accountability

| Dimension | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Accountable Org | CSU |
| Stage Status | Active / Realizing |
| Focus | Outcomes measurement, expansion |

# Stage Goal

Demonstrate measurable business value from deployed solutions, deepen customer adoption, and identify opportunities for expansion.

# Exit Criteria

- Business outcomes measured against success criteria
- Adoption targets achieved or on track
- Expansion opportunities identified and qualified
- Customer health indicators positive
- Value narrative documented for QBR delivery

# Key Activities

1. Measure business outcomes against [Customer Success Criteria](/csu/planning/customer-success-plan.md)
2. Drive usage adoption via [Usage Pipeline](/csu/pipeline/usage/)
3. Identify expansion aligned to Frontier Transformation
4. Deliver [QBR](/csu/planning/integrated-customer-planning.md) demonstrating value
5. Feed expansion back to [Stage 1](/mcem/stages/1-listen-and-consult.md) for new motions

# Handoff

Transitions to [Stage 5 — Manage & Optimize](/mcem/stages/5-manage-and-optimize.md) when value is established and focus shifts to optimization and renewal.

# Source Systems

- **[MSX Dataverse](/csu/evidence/msx-dataverse.md)** — milestone completion and value-realization progression tracked in the pipeline record.
- **[eSXP CSP](/csu/evidence/esxp-csp.md)** — success plan status and linked priorities used to track realized value against the plan.
- **[MSX Insights (MSXi)](/csu/evidence/msxi.md)** — KPI measurement, snapshot metrics, and actual outcome tracking (ACR, labour hours, close rates).
