# MCEM Stages

The five lifecycle stages of the Microsoft Customer Engagement Methodology, each with defined accountability, exit criteria, and handoff points.

* [Stage 1 — Listen & Consult](1-listen-and-consult.md) - ATU-led qualification and discovery
* [Stage 2 — Inspire & Design](2-inspire-and-design.md) - STU/DES-led solution validation and commitment
* [Stage 3 — Empower & Achieve](3-empower-and-achieve.md) - CSU-led deployment and initial value realization
* [Stage 4 — Realize Value](4-realize-value.md) - CSU-led business outcomes measurement and expansion
* [Stage 5 — Manage & Optimize](5-manage-and-optimize.md) - CSU-led continuous optimization and renewal
