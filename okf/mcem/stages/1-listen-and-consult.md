# Definition

Stage 1 — Listen & Consult is the first stage of [MCEM](/mcem/methodology.md). [ATU](/mcem/organization/atu.md) creates Stage 1 opportunities, qualifies them, and drives non-qualified production milestones toward qualification.

# Accountability

| Dimension | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Accountable Org | ATU |
| Stage Status | Unqualified |
| Pipeline Type | Non-qualified production milestones |

# Stage Goal

Understand key customer outcomes, lead with [Frontier Transformation](/mcem/frontier-transformation.md) and [Discovery Questions](/mcem/discovery-questions.md) (LOB/Industry), and drive to the correct offramp Conversation.

# Exit Criteria

- Conversations and Qualified Pipeline identified through Discovery Questions
- Outcomes identified
- Decision makers identified
- Technical blockers understood
- Approval process understood
- Budget validated
- Timing confirmed

# Handoff

Upon exit criteria satisfaction, opportunity transitions to [Stage 2 — Inspire & Design](/mcem/stages/2-inspire-and-design.md) via the [ATU-to-STU handoff](/csu/processes/atu-to-stu-handoff.md).

# Pipeline Ownership

In this stage, opportunities are owned by ATU. Milestone creation and pipeline hygiene are ATU accountabilities. See [Pipeline Ownership Model](/csu/pipeline/azure/pipeline-ownership-model.md).

# Source Systems

- **[eSXP CSP](/csu/evidence/esxp-csp.md)** — the discovery-phase success plan context, early priorities, and RAID signals that frame stage progression.
