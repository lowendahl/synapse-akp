# Definition

The Account Technology Unit (ATU) is the Microsoft field organization responsible for customer-facing account management, discovery, and pipeline creation. In MCEM, ATU is accountable for [Stage 1 — Listen & Consult](/mcem/stages/1-listen-and-consult.md).

# MCEM Accountability

| Stage | Role |
|-------|------|
| Stage 1 | **Accountable** — leads Frontier Transformation & Discovery |
| Stage 2+ | Outcome ownership across the full lifecycle (all stages) |

# Key Responsibilities

- Lead [Frontier Transformation](/mcem/frontier-transformation.md) discovery with customers
- Execute [Discovery Questions](/mcem/discovery-questions.md) to qualify intent
- Create Stage 1 opportunities in MSX
- Drive non-qualified production milestones toward qualification
- Execute [ATU-to-STU handoff](/csu/processes/atu-to-stu-handoff.md) upon qualification

# Pipeline Ownership

ATU owns unqualified pipeline and is responsible for pipeline creation, hygiene, and qualification in Stage 1.
