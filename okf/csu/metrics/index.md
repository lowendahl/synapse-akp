# CSU Metrics & KPIs

The complete set of CSU operational metrics, organized by leading/lagging classification.

## Leading Indicators (predictive — tell you where you're heading)

* [Customer Health Revenue at Risk](customer-health-rar.md) — % of revenue on customers failing the health checklist
* [Usage Revenue at Risk (M365)](usage-revenue-at-risk.md) — % of M365 revenue on under-adopted accounts
* [Cloud (ACR) Revenue at Risk](cloud-acr-revenue-at-risk.md) — Azure consumption revenue at risk from declining usage
* [MAU — Monthly Active Users](mau.md) — M365 active usage breadth
* [PAU — Productive Active Users](pau.md) — Productive active usage depth (habitual M365 use)
* [In-Quarter Create (IQC)](in-quarter-create.md) — Pipeline origination velocity within current quarter
* [Remaining Hours](remaining-hours.md) — Undelivered Enhanced Solutions hours within contract lifecycle
* [Requested Not Booked Hours](requested-not-booked.md) — Staffing demand without CSA assignment

## Lagging Indicators (outcome — tell you what already happened)

* [Job 1 — Commit to Complete (C2C)](job1-commit-to-complete.md) — % of committed milestones closed on time (95% target)
* [Job 2 — Total Completed Created](job2-completed-created.md) — CSU-originated pipeline completing in-quarter (20% target)
* [UDC — Unified Delivery Coverage](../delivery/udc.md) — % of sold proactive services delivered or booked
* [UCR — Unified Consumed Revenue](../../mcem/unified/consumed-revenue.md) — Revenue recognized from delivered hours
* [NNR — Net New Revenue (ECIF Yield)](nnr.md) — ECIF investment yield: NNR attained vs planned
* [DSAT — Customer Dissatisfaction](dsat.md) — Bottom-2-box OSAT responses on CX Pulse
* [PRU — Productive Recurring User](pru.md) — Users with ≥30 intentional actions in 28-day rolling window

## Diagnostic Indicators (operational health — neither pure leading nor lagging)

* [On-Strategy Delivery](on-strategy-delivery.md) — % of Area CSA hours aligned to FY delivery strategy
* [Booked Hours](booked-hours.md) — CSA hours scheduled against active delivery engagements
* [Repeatable Delivery](repeatable-delivery.md) — % of delivery hours using repeatable/scalable IP vs bespoke
