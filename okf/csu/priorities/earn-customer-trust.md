# Definition

Earn Customer Trust is CSU's second FY27 priority. It focuses on building deep trust through stakeholder alignment, secure resilient architectures, and a cohesive support experience.

# Key Actions

- Build executive relationships through CSP, CSDR, and QBR cadences
- Drive customer satisfaction (reduce DSAT, improve OSAT)
- Architect secure, resilient foundations
- Provide cohesive support experience across reactive and proactive motions
- Strengthen [Customer Health](/mcem/metrics/customer-health.md) through consistent engagement
