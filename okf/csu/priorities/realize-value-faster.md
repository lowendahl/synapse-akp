# Definition

Realize Value Faster is CSU's first FY27 priority. It focuses on accelerating the time-to-value for customer investments in the Microsoft Cloud by aligning [Customer Success Plans](/csu/planning/customer-success-plan.md) to measurable business outcomes.

# Key Actions

- Align CSP to customer business outcomes (not just technical deployment)
- Accelerate utilization of existing investments before pursuing expansion
- Drive adoption through [Usage Pipeline](/csu/pipeline/usage/two-motion-model.md) motions
- Leverage [Success Programs](/csu/programs/success-programs.md) to scale predictable outcomes
- Measure value through [Customer Health](/mcem/metrics/customer-health.md) signals
