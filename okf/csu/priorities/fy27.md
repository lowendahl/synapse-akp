# Definition

The five FY27 Customer Success priorities that guide all CSU execution, under the CE&S strategic objective "Accelerate customer outcomes through the Microsoft cloud."

# The Five Pillars

## 1. Realize Value Faster

Align success plans to customer business outcomes, accelerate utilization of investments, drive adoption and consumption. See [detail](/csu/priorities/realize-value-faster.md).

## 2. Earn Customer Trust

Build trust with the Microsoft Cloud, drive satisfaction, build secure resilient architectures, provide cohesive support experience. See [detail](/csu/priorities/earn-customer-trust.md).

## 3. Lead AI Transformation

Become the partner of choice for AI transformation, help customers prioritize AI use cases, modernize data and app foundations for AI readiness. See [detail](/csu/priorities/lead-ai-transformation.md).

## 4. Right People in the Right Moments with the Right Skills

Invest in Security, AI, and Industry skilling, increase technical intensity, use AI to streamline internal work. See [detail](/csu/priorities/right-people-skills.md).

## 5. Execute with Excellence

Drive execution on healthy pipeline, ensure appropriate lifecycle motions, standardize and scale repeatable outcomes. See [detail](/csu/priorities/execute-with-excellence.md).
