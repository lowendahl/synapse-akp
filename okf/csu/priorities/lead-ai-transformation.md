# Definition

Lead AI Transformation is CSU's third FY27 priority. It positions CSU as the trusted partner for customer AI transformation, directly aligned to [Frontier Transformation](/mcem/frontier-transformation.md).

# Key Actions

- Help customers prioritize high-value AI use cases
- Modernize data and application foundations for AI readiness
- Drive Copilot adoption and usage excellence
- Connect AI transformation to measurable business outcomes
- Leverage [Frontier Conversations](/mcem/frontier-transformation.md) in Stages 4–5
