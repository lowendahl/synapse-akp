# Definition

Execute with Excellence is CSU's fifth FY27 priority. It focuses on operational rigor — driving execution on healthy pipeline, ensuring appropriate lifecycle motions are triggered, and standardizing repeatable outcomes.

# Key Actions

- Drive [Commit-to-Complete](/csu/processes/commit-to-complete.md) at 95%+ completion rate
- Maintain [Pipeline Hygiene](/csu/pipeline/azure/pipeline-hygiene.md) standards
- Execute [T-minus Renewal Motions](/csu/processes/t-minus-renewal.md) on schedule
- Standardize delivery through [Programs](/csu/programs/) and playbooks
- Scale through automation and AI-assisted planning
