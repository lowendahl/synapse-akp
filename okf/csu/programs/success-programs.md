# Definition

Success Programs are scalable delivery programs that drive predictable customer outcomes through standardized engagements. They provide the "programmatic muscle" that allows CSU to scale beyond 1:1 customer engagement.

# Purpose

- Scale predictable outcomes across the customer portfolio
- Reduce dependency on individual expertise for common scenarios
- Provide structured paths for adoption, migration, and optimization
- Enable [CSAMs](/csu/roles/csam.md) to leverage programs rather than custom-building each engagement

# Types

- **Adoption programs** — structured paths to drive workload adoption
- **Migration programs** — repeatable migration execution frameworks
- **Optimization programs** — cost and performance optimization at scale
- **Security programs** — security posture improvement programs

# Relationship to Priorities

Success Programs directly support [Realize Value Faster](/csu/priorities/realize-value-faster.md) by providing the scalable delivery mechanisms that accelerate time-to-value.

# Source Systems

- **[MDM & GRM](/csu/evidence/mdm-grm.md)** — MDM provides the sold program packages and entitlements that define delivery scope.
- **[eSXP CSP](/csu/evidence/esxp-csp.md)** — success plans link those programs to customer priorities and execution tracking.
