# Definition

Unified for Partners (UfP) is a FY27 modernized, partner-led support offering sold and delivered out of SME&C. It aligns with Microsoft's partner strategy by providing Practice Building capabilities and reducing reactive demand.

# Key Changes from Legacy

| Dimension | Legacy (PSfP) | FY27 (UfP) |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Focus | Reactive support | Practice Building outcomes |
| CSAM role | Treat partner as end-customer | Drive partner capability growth |
| Delivery | Support-heavy | Enablement-heavy |
| Measurement | Ticket resolution | Partner practice maturity |

# Transition

Partner CSAMs shift focus toward Practice Building outcomes. Existing ASfP/PSfP partner duties continue until contract expiration, with gradual transition to UfP model.

# Segment

Sold and delivered out of SME&C (Small, Medium, Enterprise & Commercial).
