# Definition

Ruthless Prioritization is the operating principle that CSU must focus finite resources on the highest-impact activities, explicitly choosing what NOT to do. In a world of infinite customer needs and finite capacity, the quality of prioritization determines the quality of outcomes.

# Application

- Portfolio level: Which customers receive proactive engagement vs. programmatic coverage
- Account level: Which milestones are actively driven vs. monitored
- Activity level: Which meetings, reviews, and interventions create the most value
- Escalation level: When to escalate vs. when to accept and move on

# Connection to MCEM

Ruthless Prioritization is how CSU manages the [Commit-to-Complete](/csu/processes/commit-to-complete.md) model at scale — not every milestone receives equal attention. Resources flow to where the risk-adjusted return is highest.

# Anti-Pattern

The opposite of Ruthless Prioritization is "spreading thin" — delivering mediocre attention across all customers equally, regardless of complexity, risk, or growth potential.
