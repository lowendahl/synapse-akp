# CSU & MCEM Knowledge Bundle

A structured knowledge library covering Microsoft's Customer Success Unit (CSU) execution model and the Microsoft Customer Engagement Methodology (MCEM) framework.

## Domains

* [MCEM](mcem/) - The Microsoft Customer Engagement Methodology: stages, accountability, metrics, and risks
* [CSU](csu/) - Customer Success Unit execution: priorities, roles, pipeline, processes, programs, and doctrine

## About This Bundle

This bundle represents the authoritative knowledge corpus for how Microsoft engages customers through MCEM and how CSU executes that methodology. It is organized around organizational objects — not documents — following the Enterprise Knowledge Corpus principle that knowledge exists as things, not files.

**Scope:** FY27 (effective 2026-07-01) CSU and MCEM operating model.

**Separation of concerns:**
- `mcem/` contains the methodology itself — stages, exit criteria, accountability model, and cross-org metrics
- `csu/` contains CSU's execution layer — how CSU operationalizes MCEM through pipeline, processes, programs, and roles
